Antalya Airport to Kemer – Private Transfer Guide

Kemer is a picturesque resort town surrounded by the Taurus Mountains and the Mediterranean Sea. Known for its pine forests, clear water, and excellent all-inclusive resorts, it's a favourite among European holidaymakers. A private transfer from Antalya Airport to Kemer takes around 50 minutes.

💡 Kemer is approximately 45 km southwest of Antalya Airport. The route passes through Antalya city and takes about 45–55 minutes.
