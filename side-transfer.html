About Side

Your Guide to Getting from Antalya Airport to Side

Side is one of the most visited destinations in Turkey, known for its ancient Temple of Apollo, beautiful sandy beaches and stunning sunsets. The town sits on a small peninsula on the Manavgat River delta, surrounded by crystal-clear Mediterranean waters.

The drive from Antalya Airport to Side takes you along the D400 coastal highway — one of Turkey's most scenic routes, passing through pine forests and glimpses of the turquoise sea. Unlike a cramped shared shuttle bus, a private transfer lets you enjoy the journey in comfort.
