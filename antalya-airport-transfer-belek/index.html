How Long is the Transfer from Antalya Airport to Belek?

The distance from Antalya Airport to Belek is approximately 40 km via the D400 motorway. The journey typically takes 35 to 45 minutes depending on traffic conditions.

During summer peak season (July–August), traffic near the airport can add 5–10 minutes. Your driver will choose the fastest route.
