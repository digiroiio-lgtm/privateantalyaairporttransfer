Antalya Airport to Alanya Transfer – Full Guide

Alanya is one of Turkey's most vibrant holiday destinations, offering a stunning castle, beautiful beaches, and lively nightlife. Located approximately 130 km east of Antalya Airport, the best way to get there is by private transfer.

💡 Important: Alanya does NOT have its own commercial airport. All international flights arrive at Antalya Airport (AYT). The transfer takes around 2 hours and costs from €65.

Alanya Transfer vs Shared Shuttle: Which is Better?

For a long-distance journey like Alanya, the difference between private and shared is especially significant:

  • ✅ Private transfer: Direct, ~2 hours, comfortable, fixed price
  • ❌ Shared shuttle: 3–5 stops, 3–4 hours total, you wait for others
  • ❌ Public bus: 2.5+ hours, multiple connections, limited luggage

For a family or group, the price difference is minimal while the comfort gain is enormous.
