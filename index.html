FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking your private Antalya airport transfer.

Is Antalya airport transfer expensive?

+

Antalya airport transfers are very affordable compared to regular taxis. Private transfers start from just €35 for short distances like Lara Beach, €45 to Belek, €55 to Side and €75 to Alanya. Because we use fixed prices, you always know exactly what you will pay — making budgeting your holiday much easier. Compared to metered taxis, which can be unpredictable especially in tourist areas, a fixed-price private transfer from Antalya Airport often works out the same price or cheaper, with far better service.

How much is a taxi from Antalya Airport to Belek?

+

A private transfer from Antalya Airport to Belek costs €45 with our fixed-price service — this is the total price for the whole vehicle, not per person. Regular metered taxis from Antalya Airport to Belek typically cost between €50 and €70, depending on traffic and the driver. Our fixed-price private transfer to Belek includes a meet-and-greet service at arrivals, flight tracking, and door-to-door delivery to your hotel — all at a guaranteed price with no hidden charges.

Is private transfer better than taxi from Antalya Airport?

+

Yes — a private transfer from Antalya Airport is almost always the better option compared to a regular taxi. Here is why: Fixed prices mean you know the cost before you travel. Your driver meets you at arrivals with a name board, so there is no searching for a cab. We track your flight, so the driver waits even if you are delayed. Our vehicles are modern and air-conditioned. Drivers speak English and know every hotel and resort. There are no hidden fees for luggage, late night transfers or highway tolls.

How long does the transfer from Antalya Airport to Alanya take?

+

The transfer from Antalya Airport to Alanya takes approximately 2 to 2.5 hours, covering about 130 km along the beautiful D400 Mediterranean coastal highway. Journey times can vary depending on traffic, the specific part of Alanya you are heading to (such as Mahmutlar, Oba or town centre), and the time of day. Summer afternoons can occasionally be busier. Our drivers always take the most efficient route to minimise your travel time and ensure a comfortable journey.

Can I book an Antalya airport transfer online?

+

Yes — booking your Antalya airport transfer online is easy and takes less than 3 minutes. Simply choose your destination from our routes (Belek, Side, Alanya, Kemer, Lara Beach and more), select your preferred vehicle type, enter your flight number and arrival time, confirm your booking and pay securely online. You will receive instant confirmation by email, along with your driver's details. We recommend booking in advance to guarantee your vehicle and price, especially during the busy summer season from June to September.
