FAQ – Antalya Airport Transfers

Everything you need to know before booking your transfer.

Prices start from €20 for Antalya city centre, €35 to Belek, €45 to Side, €65 to Alanya, €40 to Kemer, and €80 to Kalkan. All prices are fixed – no hidden extras. We also offer discounts for return transfers and groups.

No prepayment is required. You pay the driver directly on arrival in cash (EUR, GBP, USD or TRY) or by card. There is absolutely no financial risk for you.

We track every flight in real time using live flight data. If your flight is delayed, your driver will automatically adjust their arrival time. There is no extra charge for waiting due to flight delays.

After booking, you will receive your driver's details and phone number via WhatsApp. Your driver will be waiting in the arrivals hall (after customs) holding a name sign with your name on it. You can also call or message the driver directly.

Yes, our transfer service operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year – including public holidays, Christmas, New Year, and Ramadan.

We offer sedans (up to 3 passengers), minivans (up to 7 passengers), and minibuses (up to 14 passengers). All vehicles are modern, air-conditioned, and well-maintained.

Yes! We offer discounts for return (round trip) bookings. Simply let us know your outbound and return dates when booking. Contact us via WhatsApp for the best price.

Absolutely. We are a fully licensed and insured transportation company registered with the Turkish Ministry of Transport. All drivers are professional, experienced, and background-checked. Our vehicles are regularly inspected for safety.
