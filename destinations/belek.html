About the Antalya Airport to Belek Transfer

Belek is one of the most popular destinations from Antalya Airport, located just 40–50 km east of the terminal. The journey takes 30–40 minutes along the D400 highway, making it one of the quickest and most straightforward airport transfers on the Turkish Riviera.

Our private transfer from Antalya Airport to Belek includes a meet-and-greet service at arrivals, free flight tracking, and door-to-door delivery to your resort or hotel. The fixed price of €45 covers the whole vehicle — not per person — making it excellent value for families and groups.
