Antalya Airport Transfer to Side – Everything You Need to Know

Side is one of Turkey's most beautiful resort towns, famous for its ancient Roman ruins, long sandy beaches, and vibrant nightlife. Getting from Antalya Airport to Side by private transfer is the most comfortable and reliable option.

💡 Did you know? Side is approximately 65 km from Antalya Airport via the D400 coastal highway. A private transfer takes around 55–65 minutes and costs from €45.
