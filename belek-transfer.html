About Belek

Antalya Airport to Belek — Your Complete Transfer Guide

Belek is Turkey's premier golf and all-inclusive resort destination, located on the Turquoise Coast just 40 km from Antalya Airport. Home to over 20 world-class golf courses and dozens of 5-star all-inclusive resorts, Belek attracts millions of tourists each year from across Europe and the world.

Getting from Antalya Airport (AYT) to Belek is straightforward — take the D400 east for about 35–40 minutes. However, navigating with luggage and potentially jet-lagged children is far easier in a private transfer than on a shared shuttle bus where you might wait an hour and stop at multiple hotels.
