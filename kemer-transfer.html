About Kemer

Antalya Airport to Kemer — What to Expect

Kemer is a picturesque resort town on the Turkish Riviera, flanked by the Taurus Mountains and the Mediterranean Sea. It's known for its stunning Blue Flag beaches, vibrant marina and the ancient Lycian ruins at Phaselis — just a short drive south.

Unlike the busier resort towns further east, Kemer has a more intimate, relaxed atmosphere. Many large all-inclusive resorts are set against the dramatic mountain backdrop, offering views that few other Turkish destinations can match.
