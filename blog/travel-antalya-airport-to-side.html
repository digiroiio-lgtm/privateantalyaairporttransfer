How to Travel from Antalya Airport to Side

Your complete transport guide for the Antalya Airport to Side journey — with prices, journey times and local tips.

Side is one of Turkey's most enchanting tourist destinations — a small historic peninsula jutting into the Mediterranean, dotted with ancient Roman ruins, beautiful sandy beaches and a lively harbour. Every year, hundreds of thousands of tourists fly into Antalya Airport and head east to Side. But what is the best way to make the Antalya Airport to Side transfer?

In this guide, we cover every transport option for travelling from Antalya Airport to Side, with honest advice on which is best for different types of travellers.

How Far is Side from Antalya Airport?

Side is located approximately 65 kilometres east of Antalya Airport (AYT). The journey takes between 55 minutes and 75 minutes depending on traffic conditions and which part of Side you are heading to. The main resort area around Side town centre and Colakli, Kumköy and Evrenseki (the larger hotel strip) can add 10–15 minutes to the journey.

The route follows the D400 highway east from Antalya, passing through Manavgat before reaching the Side peninsula. It is a well-travelled road with clear signage.

Option 2: Taxi from Antalya Airport to Side

Official taxis from Antalya Airport to Side cost approximately €65–€90 using the taximeter. Night-time rates (after midnight) are around 50% higher, making a late-night taxi to Side potentially cost €100–€135. Highway toll charges may also apply.

While taxis are reliable for short distances, for the 65 km journey to Side, the variable metered fare makes the total cost unpredictable. There is also the risk of traffic, which can add both time and money to a metered fare.

Option 3: Shared Shuttle Bus to Side

Shared shuttle services operate from Antalya Airport to the Side region. Prices are typically €10–€18 per person. The major drawback is that shared shuttles serve multiple hotels along the route, so your transfer to Side could take 90 minutes to 2.5 hours even though the direct journey is under an hour.

For solo budget travellers, a shared shuttle can make economic sense. For couples and families, the extra journey time and discomfort usually makes a private transfer a better choice at a very similar total price.

Option 4: Public Bus to Side

It is possible to reach Side from Antalya Airport by public bus, but it is complex. You first need to take the HAVAS airport bus to Antalya Otogar (main bus station), then a long-distance bus towards Manavgat, followed by a local dolmuş (minibus) to Side. The total journey can take 2.5 to 3.5 hours with connections, and it is not recommended for tourists with luggage.

Side — Why It Is Worth the Journey

Side is one of the highlights of the Turkish Riviera. The ancient ruins of the Apollo Temple at sunset are genuinely spectacular, and the beaches at Side Town and the surrounding resort villages are among the best in Turkey. The main hotel strip stretching towards Evrenseki offers hundreds of all-inclusive options at competitive prices.

Key Attractions in Side

  • Temple of Apollo — one of Turkey's most photographed ancient monuments
  • Side Antique Theatre — ancient Roman amphitheatre
  • Side Museum — Roman artefacts including stunning sculptures
  • Main Beach and East Beach — Blue Flag awarded beaches
  • Manavgat Waterfall — a short day trip from Side
  • Bazaar and harbour area — great for local shopping and dining

Tips for Your Antalya Airport to Side Transfer

Book in Advance for Summer

Side is extremely popular in July and August. Private transfers to Side should be booked well in advance during peak season to ensure vehicle availability at your arrival time.

Know Your Exact Hotel Area

The Side resort area is spread over a significant distance. "Side" can refer to the historic peninsula itself, or the larger Colakli and Evrenseki hotel zone 5–10 km to the north-east. Provide the exact hotel name when booking to ensure correct routing.

Night Arrivals

Many charter flights to Antalya arrive late at night. A pre-booked private transfer is strongly recommended for night arrivals, as taxi queues can be long and the night rate makes taxis significantly more expensive.

Frequently Asked Questions: Antalya Airport to Side

How long is the transfer from Antalya Airport to Side?

The journey from Antalya Airport to Side takes approximately 55 to 70 minutes by private transfer, covering about 65 km along the D400 highway.

How much does it cost to get from Antalya Airport to Side?

A private transfer from Antalya Airport to Side costs €55 (fixed price for the whole vehicle). Official taxis cost approximately €65–€90 using the meter.

Is there a direct bus from Antalya Airport to Side?

There is no single direct bus. Public bus travel requires connections at Antalya Otogar and Manavgat, taking 2.5–3.5 hours total. A private transfer or shared shuttle is more practical.
