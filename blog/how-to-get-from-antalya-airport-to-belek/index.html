How to Get from Antalya Airport to Belek (2026) – All Transport Options

📅 January 2026 ⏱ 5 min read ✍️ Private Antalya Transfer Team

Belek is one of Turkey's premier holiday destinations, famous for its world-class golf courses and luxury all-inclusive resorts. If you've just landed at Antalya Airport (AYT) and need to get to Belek, you have several options – but not all are equal. This guide covers everything you need to know.

📍 Antalya Airport (AYT) to Belek: ~40 km · ~40 minutes · Prices from €35 (private transfer)

Option 1: Private Transfer (Recommended)

A private transfer from Antalya Airport to Belek is by far the most popular choice among international travellers. Here's why:

  • ✅ Direct to your hotel – no stops, no detours
  • ✅ Fixed price from €35 – no meter, no surprises
  • ✅ Driver waiting at arrivals with name sign
  • ✅ No prepayment – pay on arrival
  • ✅ Available 24/7, even for late-night flights
  • ✅ Free flight tracking – driver adjusts if your flight is delayed

For a family of 4, a private transfer costs around €35–45 total, which works out cheaper per person than many alternatives when you factor in comfort and convenience.

Journey time: 35–45 minutes direct to your hotel.

Option 2: Taxi from Antalya Airport to Belek

You can take an official taxi from outside the arrivals hall at Antalya Airport. However, taxis have some downsides to be aware of:

  • ⚠️ Metered fare – final cost depends on traffic, route, and taxi company
  • ⚠️ No name sign – you need to find and negotiate with the driver
  • ⚠️ Limited luggage space (sedan taxi)
  • ⚠️ Some unlicensed touts operate near the airport – always use official taxis

Estimated cost: €35–55 (varies). Journey time: 35–45 minutes.

Option 3: Shared Shuttle Bus

Several companies operate shared shuttle buses from Antalya Airport to Belek hotels. These are the cheapest option per person but have significant drawbacks:

  • ⚠️ You wait for the bus to fill up (30–60 min wait)
  • ⚠️ Multiple stops at other hotels (adds 30–60 min to journey)
  • ⚠️ Must book in advance
  • ⚠️ Set departure times – not available 24/7

Estimated cost: €8–15 per person. Total journey time: 1.5–2.5 hours.

Option 4: Public Bus

There is no direct public bus from Antalya Airport to Belek. You would need to:

  1. Take airport bus to Antalya city centre (30 min)
  2. Take a dolmuş (shared minibus) towards Belek direction
  3. Transfer again at Serik bus stop
  4. Take another minibus to your hotel area

Total journey: 2–3 hours. Not recommended with luggage or for families.

Our Verdict: Best Way to Get from Antalya Airport to Belek

For most travellers – especially families, couples, and those with luggage – a private transfer is clearly the best option. The price difference vs a taxi is minimal, but the reliability, fixed pricing, and meet & greet service make it far superior.

Shared shuttles only make sense for solo budget travellers who are flexible with time. Public buses are only viable for those without luggage and with plenty of time.
