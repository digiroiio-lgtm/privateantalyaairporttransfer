Cheapest Way to Get from Antalya Airport to Alanya

Budget travel guide for the Antalya Airport to Alanya route — all options compared with real prices.

The journey from Antalya Airport to Alanya is one of the longest airport transfers on the Turkish Riviera — covering approximately 130 km along the stunning Mediterranean coastline. This means transport costs are higher than shorter routes like Belek or Lara Beach, which is why many tourists search for the cheapest way to get from Antalya Airport to Alanya.

In this guide, we look honestly at every option available — from public buses to private transfers — and explain which offers the best combination of value and practicality for different types of travellers.

How Far is Alanya from Antalya Airport?

Alanya is approximately 130 km east of Antalya Airport (AYT), making it the furthest major tourist resort from the airport. The journey follows the scenic D400 coastal highway and takes 2 to 2.5 hours by road. Traffic is generally light outside of peak season, but summer Saturdays (turnover day for holiday resorts) can be busier.

Alanya itself covers a large area — the distance varies depending on your specific destination within the region. Mahmutlar, Oba, Tosmur and the city centre all have slightly different journey times from the airport.

Option 2: Shared Shuttle Bus to Alanya — Budget Choice

Shared shuttle services operate between Antalya Airport and Alanya for approximately €15–€25 per person. While this sounds cheap, several things make shared shuttles less attractive for the long Alanya route:

  • The shuttle stops at multiple hotels along the way, adding significant time
  • Journey time is typically 3 to 4 hours versus 2 to 2.5 for a private transfer
  • Luggage handling with multiple stops can be inconvenient
  • You may share the bus with many other passengers

For a family of 4, the shared shuttle costs €60–€100 total — compared to €75 for a private door-to-door transfer. The private transfer wins on value for 3 or more passengers.

💡 Value tip: For groups of 3 or more travelling to Alanya, a private transfer at €75 total is almost always cheaper per person than a shared shuttle, and saves 1–1.5 hours of extra travel time.

Option 3: Official Taxi to Alanya — Most Expensive

An official metered taxi from Antalya Airport to Alanya is the most expensive option, costing approximately €130–€180 during the day and up to €200+ at night (Tariff 2). This makes the taxi the least cost-effective choice for the Alanya route by a significant margin.

The only scenario where a taxi to Alanya makes sense is if you need immediate transport on arrival and have not pre-booked anything. Even then, a shared shuttle is much cheaper.

Option 4: Public Bus — Cheapest but Most Complicated

The absolute cheapest way to get from Antalya Airport to Alanya by public transport costs approximately €6–€12 per person, but involves multiple connections and is not practical for most tourists:

  1. Take the HAVAS airport bus to Antalya Otogar (central bus station) — about 30 minutes, €3
  2. Buy a ticket on a long-distance coach to Alanya — approximately €7–€10, takes about 2.5 hours
  3. On arrival at Alanya bus station, take a local taxi to your hotel

Total journey time including connections and waiting: 3.5–5 hours. This is fine for backpackers with light luggage, but impractical for families or those with significant luggage.

Option 5: Car Hire from Antalya Airport

If you plan to explore the Alanya region independently, car hire from Antalya Airport offers flexibility. Daily rates start from about €35 for a basic model. The drive to Alanya along the D400 is scenic and not difficult. However, you then have to return the car at the end of your holiday (either to Antalya Airport or from Alanya — the latter may involve a drop-off fee).

About Alanya — Why It Is Worth the Journey

Alanya is one of Turkey's most iconic tourist destinations. The centrepiece is the spectacular Alanya Castle, a medieval fortress perched on a rocky peninsula 250 metres above the Mediterranean, offering panoramic views that are simply unforgettable.

The resort offers a perfect combination of history, beaches and nightlife. Cleopatra Beach — named after the legendary Egyptian queen who allegedly swam there with Mark Antony — is one of Turkey's finest stretches of sand. The Dim Cave and Dim River are popular day trips, and the city centre has excellent restaurants and shopping.

Mahmutlar, Oba and Konakli to the east of Alanya centre offer more affordable accommodation options and are popular with budget-conscious tourists from Scandinavia and Germany.

Whatever part of Alanya you are heading to, a private transfer from Antalya Airport will get you there comfortably and affordably.
