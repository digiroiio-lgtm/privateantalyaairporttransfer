Is Antalya Airport Transfer Safe for Tourists?

Everything you need to know about staying safe when using airport transfers in Antalya, Turkey.

Safety is a top concern for many tourists visiting Turkey for the first time. When you land at Antalya Airport after a long flight, the last thing you want is to be taken advantage of by an unscrupulous driver or overcharged for your transfer. So the question is a valid one: Is Antalya airport transfer safe for tourists?

The short answer is yes — Antalya airport transfers are generally very safe, particularly when you use licensed operators and pre-booked services. In this guide, we explain everything you need to know to have a safe and trouble-free transfer from Antalya Airport.

Is Antalya Airport Transfer Generally Safe?

Antalya is Turkey's most visited tourist destination, welcoming over 15 million foreign visitors every year. The city's entire economy is built around tourism, which means the airport transfer industry is well-regulated, professionally managed, and heavily used by tourists from across Europe.

The vast majority of tourists who use airport transfers in Antalya — whether by pre-booked private transfer or official taxi — have completely uneventful, safe journeys. Turkey has strong consumer protection laws and the Turkish tourism industry has every incentive to maintain its reputation for safety and hospitality.

What Are the Real Risks at Antalya Airport?

While Antalya is generally safe, there are specific risks that tourists should be aware of:

1. Unofficial Taxi Touts

Inside the Antalya Airport arrivals hall, you may be approached by individuals offering taxi or transfer services. These are often unlicensed operators who may charge inflated prices, use unsafe vehicles, or in rare cases, take tourists to unofficial accommodation or timeshare presentations.

How to avoid: Ignore any approaches inside the terminal. If you need a taxi, use only the official yellow taxis from the designated rank outside the arrivals hall.

2. Unmetered or Dishonest Taxis

While official Antalya taxis are licensed and use taximeters, occasional complaints arise about drivers who claim the meter is broken, quote a flat rate higher than the metered price would be, or add unexplained charges at the end of the journey.

How to avoid: Always insist on the meter being used from the start of the journey. Confirm the correct tariff (Tariff 1 during the day, Tariff 2 after midnight). Ask about toll charges before you start.

3. Overcharging at Night

Antalya taxis are legally permitted to charge 50% more after midnight (Tariff 2). This is legitimate but comes as a shock to many tourists. A taxi to Alanya that costs €150 in the afternoon may cost €200+ at 2 AM.

How to avoid: Pre-book a private transfer with a fixed price. Our fixed prices apply 24/7, with no night-rate surcharge.

4. Fake Transfer Companies Online

When searching for Antalya airport transfers online, you may encounter websites from unreliable companies that take your booking and payment but fail to provide the service. This is rare but does happen, particularly with very cheap offers.

How to avoid: Book with established, well-reviewed companies. Look for genuine customer reviews, a clear refund and cancellation policy, and responsive customer service. Our company has over 847 verified reviews.

How to Choose a Safe Antalya Airport Transfer Provider

Check for Licensing

A legitimate Antalya airport transfer company will be registered with Turkish transport authorities. Drivers should carry valid professional driving licences and commercial vehicle permits. If you are unsure, you can ask to see the driver's credentials.

Look for Real Reviews

Check reviews on independent platforms like TripAdvisor, Google Reviews and Trustpilot. A company with hundreds of genuine positive reviews from customers across multiple countries is a reliable indicator of a trustworthy service.

Fixed Prices vs Meters

Companies offering fixed-price transfers provide significantly more price transparency than metered services. When your total cost is fixed before you travel, there is no room for fare manipulation.

Verifiable Contact Details

Legitimate transfer companies will have a local phone number, email address and WhatsApp contact — and actually respond to messages before your transfer. If you cannot get a response before you travel, consider using a different provider.

Driver Information in Advance

A good private transfer company will send you your driver's name, photo and vehicle details before you arrive. This lets you verify that the person meeting you at arrivals is your booked driver.

Antalya is one of the safest tourist destinations in Turkey for solo female travellers. The transfer industry caters to solo travellers from across Europe daily, and professional licensed drivers are accustomed to and trained for this. Several things make solo female travel safer:

  • Pre-booking means you know who your driver is before you land
  • Share your driver's details with a friend or family member before travel
  • Our drivers carry official ID and can show credentials on request
  • Our 24/7 customer support can be contacted any time during your journey

Using a pre-booked private Antalya airport transfer is actually safer for solo travellers than taking a random taxi, because of the accountability and pre-vetting of drivers.

What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

In the unlikely event of a problem with your airport transfer, here are the steps to take:

  1. Note the vehicle registration number and driver details
  2. Contact your transfer company's 24/7 support line immediately
  3. If you feel unsafe, call the Turkish emergency number: 155 (Police) or 112 (Emergency)
  4. For official taxis, you can report complaints to the taxi regulator via the airport
  5. Document any issues with photos or screenshots for any subsequent complaint
