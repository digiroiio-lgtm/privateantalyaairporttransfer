How Far is Belek from Antalya Airport?

Belek is one of the closest major resorts to Antalya Airport, making it a very straightforward transfer. The distance from Antalya Airport to Belek is approximately 40–50 km depending on your exact hotel location. The journey takes between 25 and 40 minutes under normal traffic conditions.

The route takes you east along the D400 highway, passing through the Aksu district before reaching the resort area of Belek. The road is well maintained and the transfer is easy even at night.

Option 2: Antalya Airport Taxi to Belek

You can take an official taxi from the taxi rank outside Antalya Airport's arrivals hall. The Antalya airport taxi to Belek uses a meter and costs approximately €50–€70 depending on traffic and your hotel's exact location. Taxis can also charge for highway tolls.

While official taxis are safe and reliable, they are generally more expensive than pre-booked private transfers for groups of 2 or more. Drivers may also have limited English language skills. There can be long queues at the taxi rank during peak summer months.

How to Find an Official Taxi at Antalya Airport

Official taxis are bright yellow and wait outside the arrivals hall. Always use the official taxi rank and never accept offers from unofficial drivers inside the terminal. The meters should start at a set tariff — confirm this before getting in.

Option 3: Shared Shuttle Bus to Belek

Several companies operate shared shuttle services from Antalya Airport to Belek. Prices range from €8 to €15 per person. The major downside is that shared shuttles stop at multiple hotels, meaning your journey could take 90 minutes to 2 hours even though Belek is only 40 km away.

Shared shuttles are suitable for solo budget travellers, but for families or couples, the extra time and inconvenience rarely makes them worth the saving. A private transfer costs the same or less for 2 or more people.

Option 4: Public Bus from Antalya Airport to Belek

The cheapest way to get from Antalya Airport to Belek by public transport involves taking the HAVAS airport bus into Antalya city centre, then connecting to a dolmuş (shared minibus) heading east towards Serik and Belek. This journey typically takes 1.5 to 2.5 hours and requires local knowledge to navigate connections. It is not recommended for tourists arriving with luggage.

Option 5: Car Hire from Antalya Airport

Car hire is available at Antalya Airport from all major rental companies. If you plan to explore the Belek region and surrounding areas independently, a hire car can be good value. However, for a straight transfer from the airport to your hotel, it is rarely the most convenient choice — you have to deal with unfamiliar roads, parking at your resort, and returning the car at the end of your holiday.

Tips for Your Antalya Airport to Belek Transfer

During peak summer months (June–September), demand for private transfers from Antalya Airport is very high. Pre-booking ensures you have a confirmed driver and vehicle waiting when you land — particularly important for late-night arrivals.

Provide Your Exact Hotel Name

Belek has many luxury hotels and golf resorts. When booking your transfer, provide the exact name and, if possible, the address of your hotel to ensure your driver takes you directly to the right entrance.

Include Your Flight Number

Always provide your flight number when booking. This allows us to track your flight and ensure your driver is waiting for you even if your flight arrives early or is delayed by several hours.

Travelling with Golf Equipment?

Belek is Turkey's golf capital — if you are travelling with golf bags, let us know when booking. We will ensure a vehicle with sufficient boot space is reserved for you. Golf bags can typically be accommodated in our minivan transfers.
