How Does the Taxi System Work at Antalya Airport?

Antalya Airport has an official taxi rank located directly outside the arrivals hall at both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. All official taxis in Antalya are bright yellow and operated by licensed drivers. The fares are calculated using a taximeter (taxi metre), which starts running as soon as you set off.

There are two tariff rates for Antalya taxis:

  • Tariff 1 (Gündüz) — daytime rate, applies between 06:00 and 24:00
  • Tariff 2 (Gece) — night rate, applies between 24:00 and 06:00, approximately 50% more expensive

Highway tolls may also be added to the fare on certain routes. Always ensure the driver sets the meter at the correct tariff before departure.

Why Are Antalya Airport Taxi Prices So Variable?

Metered taxi fares in Antalya can vary considerably for the same journey because of several factors:

Traffic Conditions

During peak summer months, traffic around Antalya can significantly extend journey times — and since taxis charge by both distance and time when stationary, a traffic jam means a more expensive fare. This is particularly relevant on routes to Alanya and Side.

Night Rate Surcharges

If your flight arrives after midnight, Antalya taxi drivers are entitled to charge Tariff 2, which is approximately 50% more expensive than the daytime rate. If you land at 2 AM, your transfer to Alanya by taxi could cost up to €200.

Highway Tolls

On some routes, especially towards Alanya, there are highway toll booths. Some drivers add these to the fare without prior notice. Always ask before you start the journey.

Unofficial Taxis and Touts

Inside the airport arrivals hall, you may be approached by unofficial taxi drivers or transfer agents offering seemingly cheap prices. These are unregulated and can charge far higher prices once you are in the vehicle. Always use the official yellow taxis from the designated rank outside the terminal.

How to Avoid Being Overcharged at Antalya Airport

1. Always Use Official Taxis

Only use the official yellow taxis from the rank outside arrivals. Never accept offers from touts inside the terminal building.

2. Confirm the Tariff Before Starting

Check that the driver sets the correct tariff (Tariff 1 during daytime, Tariff 2 at night) and that the meter starts at the base rate before setting off.

3. Agree on Toll Charges Upfront

Ask whether the fare includes highway tolls, especially on routes to Alanya, Side and Kemer. Get this confirmed before starting the journey.

Are Unofficial Taxis at Antalya Airport Safe?

Unofficial taxi drivers operating inside Antalya Airport should be avoided. While most transfers are safe, using unlicensed vehicles creates risks including overcharging, poor-condition vehicles, uninsured driving and potential safety issues. Turkish consumer protection authorities have cracked down on illegal taxi touts in recent years, but they still operate especially during peak season.

If you are ever unsure about a driver's legitimacy, ask to see their official taxi licence and ensure you are getting into an official yellow vehicle with a working taximeter.
