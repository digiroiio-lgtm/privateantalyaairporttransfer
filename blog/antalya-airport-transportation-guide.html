Antalya Airport Transportation Guide

Every transport option from Antalya Airport explained — private transfers, taxis, buses, shuttles and car hire, all in one place.

Antalya Airport (IATA: AYT) is one of the busiest airports in Europe during summer, handling over 30 million passengers every year. The vast majority are holiday tourists heading to resorts along the Turkish Riviera. Getting from the airport to your hotel is the first challenge of your holiday, and with so many transport options available, it can be confusing to know which to choose.

This comprehensive Antalya Airport transportation guide covers every option in full — private airport transfers, official taxis, shared shuttle buses, public buses and car hire — so you can make the best choice for your trip.

About Antalya Airport

Antalya Airport has two terminals:

  • Terminal 1 (Domestic) — handles Turkish domestic flights and some charter flights
  • Terminal 2 (International) — handles the majority of international flights, including all major European routes

Most tourists arriving from the UK, Germany, Netherlands, Scandinavia, Russia and other European countries will arrive at Terminal 2. Both terminals have taxi ranks, private transfer meeting points, and shuttle bus services.

A private Antalya airport transfer is the most popular transport option for tourists, and for good reason. Option 2: Official Taxis

Official yellow taxis operate 24/7 from ranks outside both terminals at Antalya Airport. All licensed taxis use metered fares and are regulated by the Antalya municipality.

Antalya Airport Taxi Prices

  • Lara Beach: €25–€40 (daytime)
  • Belek: €50–€70 (daytime)
  • Side: €65–€90 (daytime)
  • Kemer: €55–€75 (daytime)
  • Alanya: €130–€180 (daytime)

Night-time rates (Tariff 2, after midnight) are approximately 50% higher. Always confirm the tariff before starting your journey.

Best for: Solo travellers, last-minute arrivals, short distances near the airport

Option 3: Shared Shuttle Buses

Several companies operate shared shuttle buses from Antalya Airport to major resort areas. Passengers are grouped by destination and drop-offs are made sequentially at each hotel.

Shared Shuttle Prices

  • Lara Beach / Antalya: €5–€10/person
  • Belek: €8–€15/person
  • Side: €10–€18/person
  • Kemer: €8–€14/person
  • Alanya: €15–€25/person

While the per-person price is lower, the total journey time is significantly longer because the shuttle stops at every hotel. For Alanya, a shared shuttle can take 3–4 hours versus 2–2.5 hours for a private transfer.

Best for: Solo budget travellers, those not in a hurry

Option 4: HAVAS Airport Bus

HAVAS operates an express bus service between Antalya Airport and Antalya city centre (the main MIGROS shopping centre and Antalya Otogar/bus station). This service is primarily designed for Antalya city travellers, not for resort-bound tourists.

  • Price: Approximately €2–€3
  • Journey time to city centre: 30–40 minutes
  • Frequency: Every 30 minutes during the day

From the city bus station, you can connect to long-distance coaches heading east towards Side, Alanya and Kemer, or west towards Kemer. This is the cheapest option for budget backpackers, but involves multiple connections and is impractical for tourists with significant luggage.

Best for: Backpackers, budget solo travellers heading to Antalya city

Option 5: Car Hire at Antalya Airport

All major car rental companies are represented at Antalya Airport — Hertz, Avis, Europcar, Budget, Sixt and local Turkish companies. The rental desks are located in both terminal arrivals halls and in a dedicated rental car centre nearby.

Car Hire at Antalya Airport — Key Points

  • Daily rates from approximately €25–€40 for economy cars (summer rates higher)
  • A credit card is required for the security deposit
  • You will need a valid driving licence — most European licences are accepted
  • GPS is available as an add-on or use your phone navigation
  • Roads in the region are generally in good condition
  • Driving in Antalya city can be challenging — resort areas are easier

Best for: Independent travellers who want to explore the region, longer stays, those staying at multiple locations

Practical Tips for Antalya Airport Arrivals

Tip 1: Pre-Book Your Transfer

Book your Antalya airport transfer before you travel to eliminate stress on arrival and guarantee availability, especially during the busy summer season.

Tip 2: Download an Offline Map

Download an offline map of Antalya and your destination on Google Maps or Maps.me before you fly. This is useful if you need to communicate your destination to a driver.

Tip 3: Have Your Hotel Details Ready

Know the exact name and address of your hotel before landing. Taxi and transfer drivers need this information, and having it readily available saves time and avoids confusion.

Tip 4: Exchange Currency or Use ATMs at the Airport

While many transfers now accept card payments, it is useful to have some Turkish Lira (TRY) or Euros in cash. There are ATMs and currency exchange facilities inside both terminals.

Tip 5: Confirm Your Booking Before You Fly

If you have pre-booked a private transfer, confirm your booking 24 hours before departure by email or WhatsApp. This ensures your driver is fully briefed and any last-minute changes can be accommodated.

Antalya Airport: Key Facts for Tourists

  • IATA Code: AYT
  • Location: 13 km east of Antalya city centre
  • Terminals: Terminal 1 (domestic), Terminal 2 (international)
  • Annual passengers: ~35 million (peak summer)
  • Languages spoken: Turkish, English (widely), German, Russian
  • Currency: Turkish Lira (TRY). Euro accepted by most transfer services.
  • Emergency: 112 (general), 155 (police)
