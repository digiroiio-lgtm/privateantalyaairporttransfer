Is Antalya Airport Transfer Safe? Everything You Need to Know (2026)

📅 January 2026 ⏱ 5 min read

Safety is a top priority for travellers arriving at a foreign airport. If you're wondering whether Antalya Airport transfers are safe, the short answer is: yes – if you book with a licensed and reputable company. This guide explains what to look for and what to avoid.

✅ Short answer: A pre-booked private transfer with a licensed company is the safest way to get from Antalya Airport to your hotel. Always book in advance with a verified provider.

Is Turkey Safe for Tourists?

Turkey is one of the world's most popular tourist destinations, welcoming over 50 million visitors per year. Antalya is a major international tourist hub and is generally very safe for tourists. The airport area and main resort regions (Belek, Side, Alanya, Kemer) all have a strong tourist infrastructure.

That said, as with any busy international airport, there are opportunistic touts and unlicensed operators who target arriving tourists. Knowing how to recognise them is the key to staying safe.

Safety Risks at Antalya Airport – What to Watch For

1. Unlicensed Taxis and Touts

As you exit arrivals, you may be approached by people offering "cheap transfers" or "private taxis." These are often unlicensed operators who:

  • ⚠️ Charge inflated prices (often 2–3x official rates)
  • ⚠️ Are not registered or insured
  • ⚠️ May take unnecessarily long routes
  • ⚠️ Vehicles may not be roadworthy or maintained

How to avoid: Never accept a transfer offer from someone approaching you in the arrivals hall. Only use pre-booked or official services.

2. Overcharging by Unregistered Operators

Some operators advertise low prices online but add hidden charges at the airport. This is known as "bait and switch."

How to avoid: Always confirm the final price in writing (e.g., via WhatsApp) before travel. With a reputable company, the price never changes.

3. Online Scam Bookings

Some fraudulent websites take payment but never send a driver. This is increasingly rare but does happen.

How to avoid: Book with a company that does NOT require prepayment. If no prepayment is needed, there's no financial risk even in the worst case.

Safety Tips for Your Antalya Airport Arrival

  1. Book in advance – a pre-booked transfer is always safer than an impulse decision at the airport
  2. Save your driver's number – contact them before you land if needed
  3. Look for your name sign – your driver will be in the arrivals hall with a name sign
  4. Ignore touts – walk past anyone approaching you offering transfers
  5. Use official areas – if taking a taxi, use the official taxi rank outside Terminal 1 or 2
  6. Share your trip details – send your itinerary to a trusted contact before travel

Verdict: Is Antalya Airport Transfer Safe?

Yes – when booked with a licensed, reputable company, an Antalya Airport transfer is completely safe and one of the most convenient ways to start your Turkish holiday. The key is to book in advance, confirm your price in writing, and use a company with strong reviews and proper licensing.

Avoid accepting offers from touts at the airport, and never pay upfront to an unknown online operator.
