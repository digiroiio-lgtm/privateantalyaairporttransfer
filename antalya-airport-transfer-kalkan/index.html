Antalya Airport to Kalkan – Private Transfer Guide

Kalkan is one of Turkey's most exclusive and charming resort towns – a whitewashed hillside village overlooking a beautiful blue bay. It's a favourite of discerning travellers who appreciate boutique hotels, excellent restaurants, and crystal-clear water. Located approximately 200 km west of Antalya Airport along the spectacular Turquoise Coast.

💡 Note: Kalkan is only accessible by private transfer or car hire. There is no direct public transport from Antalya Airport to Kalkan. A private transfer is the only practical option.

What to Expect on the Journey

The transfer to Kalkan is a scenic 2.5-hour drive along the stunning Turquoise Coast. You'll pass through Antalya city, Kemer, and the breathtaking Taurus Mountain coastal road (the famous "D400"). Many passengers consider this drive one of the most beautiful in Turkey.

  • ⛰️ Dramatic Taurus Mountain scenery
  • 🏖️ Pass through Kemer, Olimpos, and Finike
