Alanya is 130 km east of Antalya Airport, making it the longest of our major transfer routes. The scenic D400 coastal highway takes you through pine forests, past ancient ruins and along stunning turquoise coastline. The journey takes 90–120 minutes depending on your exact destination within Alanya province.

About Alanya

Getting from Antalya Airport to Alanya

Alanya is one of Turkey's most popular tourist resorts, famous for its dramatic clifftop castle, Cleopatra Beach, Damlataş Cave and vibrant nightlife. The city stretches along the coast and is surrounded by the Taurus Mountains, offering stunning scenery at every turn.

The 130 km journey from Antalya Airport to Alanya is best done by private transfer. A shared shuttle bus stops at multiple hotels and can easily add 30–60 minutes to your journey. With us, it's direct from the airport to your door.
